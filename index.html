Our Heritage

A name that outlived the world that made it

Lustria Corporation was once the most celebrated locomotive and vehicle manufacturer in the Republic of Orvella. Its engines carried grain, ore and people across the nation, and its works shaped the towns around them. Then the vorax fungus blighted the world’s crops, famine bred war, and on 26 November 1998 the bombs fell. The survivors sheltered in the vaults.

A hundred years on, with Vault E preparing to unseal, the founders’ heir and the heir of a partner family are taking up the legacy together. They will not rebuild the old trade. Lustria will be a new enterprise, built on the same craftsmanship and ambition, and just as vital to the survival of Orvella.

The Long Road

From Republic to Vault to Surface

1984–91

The Vorax

A crop-destroying fungus appears in Fölterme and spreads worldwide. Orvella introduces rationing in 1989, and in 1991 a global famine is declared.

1991–94

Seeds and Vaults

The Agricultural Preservation Initiative builds vaults to protect seed stocks. Midway through, they are redesigned to shelter people as well.

1994–98

The War

A raid on Retuid food stores ignites a chain of alliances and a world war. Orvella’s command collapses into civil war, and on 26 November 1998 the nuclear exchange ends the old world.

1998

Vault E Opens

Survivors flood into the vaults. Under Overseer Amedeo, Vault E begins a hundred years of sealed life.

1998–2098

A Century Below

Overseers rise and fall. The Dead Air Incident, the Hydroponics Famine and the East Wing Fire test the vault, and all who remember the surface pass away.

Now

The Unsealing

Vault E announces its opening. Charterholders prepare to leave with GENESIS settlement kits. Lustria Corporation goes with them to explore, research, trade and build.

The Founders

Two Houses, One Corporation

The Volkovs, a line of scientists, metalworkers and researchers, and the family of lawyers and businessmen beside them once allied to form the greatest corporation in Orvella’s history. A hundred years later, their heirs carry it forward.

Mikhail Volkov

Age 26 · House Volkov · Science & Engineering

Mikhail is hardly a man of discipline. He is found roaming the Vault’s halls, charming company, chasing romances that never end well, and wading into affairs that are none of his business. Yet beneath the carefree air lies a first-rate education in chemistry, engineering and the workings of machinery.

Heir to a longstanding family mandate, he hopes to turn that knowledge toward rebuilding the enterprise his family once helped to rule. With the Vaults preparing to open, he is eager for a world beyond the walls to explore, experiment with and perhaps make something worthwhile of his life.
